<h2 id="hld-overview" style="color:#1e293b;font-weight:700;margin:28px 0 10px">What are Holdings?</h2>

<div style="background:#fff7ed;border-left:4px solid #ff6b00;border-radius:0 10px 10px 0;padding:16px 20px;margin-bottom:20px;font-size:14px;color:#374151;line-height:1.8">
<strong>Holdings</strong> represent the <strong>shares that you own in your Demat account after successful delivery</strong>. Unlike positions, holdings <strong>do not include intraday trades</strong> — they only show shares that have been settled and delivered to your Demat account.
</div>

<h2 id="hld-how-works" style="color:#1e293b;font-weight:700;margin:28px 0 14px">How Holdings Work</h2>

<div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;padding:20px 24px;margin-bottom:20px">
  <p style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:14px;color:#374151">When you buy shares for <strong>delivery (CNC)</strong>, the shares are credited to your Demat account on the settlement day. Once credited, they appear in your <strong>Holdings</strong> and you can:</p>
  <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,1fr);gap:10px">
    <div style="background:#fff7ed;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 14px;text-align:center">
      <div style="font-size:22px;font-weight:800;color:#ff6b00;margin-bottom:4px">Days</div>
      <div style="font-size:12px;color:#6b7280">Hold for a few days</div>
    </div>
    <div style="background:#fff7ed;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 14px;text-align:center">
      <div style="font-size:22px;font-weight:800;color:#ff6b00;margin-bottom:4px">Months</div>
      <div style="font-size:12px;color:#6b7280">Hold for several months</div>
    </div>
    <div style="background:#fff7ed;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 14px;text-align:center">
      <div style="font-size:22px;font-weight:800;color:#ff6b00;margin-bottom:4px">Years</div>
      <div style="font-size:12px;color:#6b7280">Hold for many years</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p style="margin:12px 0 0;font-size:13px;color:#6b7280">There is <strong>no requirement to sell them on the same day</strong> — unlike intraday positions.</p>
</div>
