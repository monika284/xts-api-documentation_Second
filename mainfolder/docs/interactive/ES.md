<h1 id="exchange-status" style="color:#ff6b00;font-weight:800;margin-bottom:6px">Exchange Status</h1>
<p style="color:#6b7280;font-size:15px;margin-bottom:18px"><strong>Exchange Status</strong> shows the <strong>current operating state of a stock exchange</strong> — whether it is open, closed, halted, or in a special trading session. It helps traders know if trading is currently allowed and what phase the market is in.</p>

<h2 id="es-types" style="color:#1e293b;font-weight:700;margin:28px 0 14px">Common Exchange Status Types</h2>

<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:14px;margin-bottom:24px">

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">1. Open</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px">
      <p style="margin:0 0 10px;font-size:13px;color:#374151">The exchange is open and <strong>trading is taking place normally</strong>.</p>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#4ec9b0">OPEN</span>
      </div>
      <div style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:6px">
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:3px 10px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Buy shares</span>
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:3px 10px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Sell shares</span>
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:3px 10px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Modify orders</span>
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:3px 10px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Cancel orders</span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">2. Closed</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px">
      <p style="margin:0 0 10px;font-size:13px;color:#374151">The exchange is <strong>not accepting trades</strong> — market has closed or it is a holiday.</p>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#ce9178">CLOSED</span>
      </div>
      <p style="margin:0;font-size:12px;color:#374151">No trading is possible until the next trading session.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">3. Pre-Open</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px">
      <p style="margin:0 0 10px;font-size:13px;color:#374151">A session <strong>before regular market hours</strong> where orders can be placed but continuous trading has not started yet.</p>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#4ec9b0">PRE-OPEN</span>
      </div>
      <p style="margin:0;font-size:12px;color:#374151">Orders can be entered/modified/cancelled. The exchange determines the <strong>opening price</strong>.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">4. Post-Close</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px">
      <p style="margin:0 0 10px;font-size:13px;color:#374151">A session <strong>after regular market hours</strong> where certain activities may still be allowed.</p>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#ce9178">POST-CLOSE</span>
      </div>
      <p style="margin:0;font-size:12px;color:#374151">Depending on exchange rules, some order-related activities may still be available.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">5. Trading Halt</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px">
      <p style="margin:0 0 8px;font-size:13px;color:#374151">Trading has been <strong>temporarily suspended</strong>. Reasons may include:</p>
      <div style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:5px;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:2px 9px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Corporate announcements</span>
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:2px 9px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Technical issues</span>
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:2px 9px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Regulatory actions</span>
        <span style="background:#fff7ed;border:1px solid #fed7aa;border-radius:5px;padding:2px 9px;font-size:11px;color:#c2410c;font-weight:600">Circuit breakers</span>
      </div>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#ce9178">HALTED</span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">6. Auction Session</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px">
      <p style="margin:0 0 10px;font-size:13px;color:#374151">A <strong>special session</strong> used for auction-related trading, such as settling short deliveries.</p>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#dcdcaa">AUCTION</span>
      </div>
      <p style="margin:0;font-size:12px;color:#374151">Only <strong>auction-related trades</strong> are processed during this session.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#fff;border:1.5px solid #e5e7eb;border-radius:12px;overflow:hidden;grid-column:1 / -1">
    <div style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#ff7b00,#ff9500);padding:12px 16px;display:flex;align-items:center;gap:10px">
      <strong style="color:#fff;font-size:13px">7. Suspended</strong>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:14px 16px;display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:14px;align-items:center">
      <p style="margin:0;font-size:13px;color:#374151">Trading for a specific security has been <strong>suspended until further notice</strong>. No orders can be executed for that security until the exchange lifts the suspension.</p>
      <div style="background:#1e1e1e;border-radius:7px;padding:10px 13px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;font-size:12px;color:#d4d4d4">
        <span style="color:#9cdcfe">Exchange Status</span><span style="color:#d4d4d4">: </span><span style="color:#ce9178">SUSPENDED</span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

</div>
