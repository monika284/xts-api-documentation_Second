  <p class="dep-hero-sub">Market Depth provides <strong>real-time visibility into the full order book</strong> — all pending buy and sell orders at every price level. It shows market liquidity, supply and demand, and potential price pressure far beyond the last traded price.</p>

<h2 id="what-is-market-depth" class="dep-section-title">What is Market Depth?</h2>
<p class="dep-text">The <strong>order book</strong> is a real-time list of every pending buy and sell order for an instrument. Market Depth shows you the top 5 levels on each side — price, quantity waiting at that price, and how many individual orders are queued there.</p>
<div class="dep-concepts-row">
  <div class="dep-concept-card dep-cc-bids">
    <div class="dep-cc-icon dep-ci-bids"></div>
    <h4>Bids (Buy Orders)</h4>
    <p>Pending orders from buyers. Listed from highest to lowest price. The top bid is the highest a buyer will pay right now.</p>
    <div class="dep-cc-pill dep-pill-green">Green Side</div>
  </div>
  <div class="dep-concept-card dep-cc-spread">
    <div class="dep-cc-icon dep-ci-spread"></div>
    <h4>Spread</h4>
    <p>The gap between the best ask and the best bid. A smaller spread means more buyers and sellers are actively trading.</p>
    <div class="dep-cc-pill dep-pill-gray">Center Gap</div>
  </div>
  <div class="dep-concept-card dep-cc-asks">
    <div class="dep-cc-icon dep-ci-asks"></div>
    <h4>Asks (Sell Orders)</h4>
    <p>Pending orders from sellers. Listed from lowest to highest price. The top ask is the minimum price a seller will accept.</p>
    <div class="dep-cc-pill dep-pill-red">Red Side</div>
  </div>
</div>

<h2 id="liquidity" class="dep-section-title">Reading Market Liquidity</h2>
<p class="dep-text">The depth and size of the order book tells you how easily you can execute a large order without moving the market price.</p>

<h2 id="support-resistance" class="dep-section-title">Identifying Support and Resistance</h2>
<p class="dep-text">Large concentrations of orders at specific price levels can act as <strong>support</strong> (large buy orders) or <strong>resistance</strong> (large sell orders).</p>
<div class="dep-sr-row">
  <div class="dep-sr-card dep-sr-support">
    <div class="dep-sr-title">Support Level</div>
    <div class="dep-sr-desc">A price where a significant number of buy orders are stacked. The market often bounces back up when it reaches this level because buyers absorb selling pressure.</div>
    <div class="dep-sr-example">
      <div class="dep-sre-row dep-sre-light">2965.30 — 1,800 qty</div>
      <div class="dep-sre-row dep-sre-light">2965.20 — 850 qty</div>
      <div class="dep-sre-highlight dep-sre-support">2965.00 — 12,000 qty &larr; Support</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="dep-sr-card dep-sr-resistance">
    <div class="dep-sr-title">Resistance Level</div>
    <div class="dep-sr-desc">A price where a significant number of sell orders are stacked. The market often struggles to break above this level because sellers keep absorbing buying pressure.</div>
    <div class="dep-sr-example">
      <div class="dep-sre-highlight dep-sre-resistance">2970.00 — 15,000 qty &larr; Resistance</div>
      <div class="dep-sre-row dep-sre-light">2969.50 — 600 qty</div>
      <div class="dep-sre-row dep-sre-light">2969.00 — 900 qty</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<h2 id="best-practices" class="dep-section-title">Best Practices</h2>
<div class="dep-practices-grid">
  <div class="dep-practice">
    <h4>Combine with Quote Data</h4>
    <p>Use market depth alongside the last traded price and OHLC to get the complete market picture before placing an order.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dep-practice">
    <h4>Check Liquidity Before Large Orders</h4>
    <p>Before placing a large order, verify there is enough quantity at the top levels. Thin books can cause significant slippage.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dep-practice">
    <h4>Use WebSocket for Live Updates</h4>
    <p>Subscribe to event code <code>1502</code> via WebSocket instead of polling. Order books update many times per second.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dep-practice">
    <h4>Watch for Order Book Imbalance</h4>
    <p>A large imbalance in total bid vs ask quantity often signals short-term price direction — more bids = buy pressure, more asks = sell pressure.</p>
  </div>
</div>
