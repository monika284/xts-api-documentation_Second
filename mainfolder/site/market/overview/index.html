    Market Data API Overview

    The Market Data API provides real-time and historical market information for a wide range of financial instruments.

    What is Market Data?

    Market data is information generated by exchanges during trading hours. It helps you track live prices, analyze trends, and make informed trading decisions.

    RELIANCE • NSE
    ₹2,850.00
    +25.50 (0.90%) ↗
    Open2,820.00
    High2,870.00
    Low2,810.00
    Prev. Close2,824.50
    Volume1,200,000

    Types of Market Data

    Live Data

    Real-time data received instantly from exchanges.

    Example: LTP, Bid, Ask, Volume

    Delayed Data

    Data received with a short delay (e.g., 15 minutes).

    Example: 15-min Delayed Data

    Historical Data

    Previously recorded data for past dates.

    Example: Daily, Weekly, Monthly

    Key Features

    Real-time Quotes

    Get the latest price, change, volume and other market details in real-time.

    Historical Data

    Access historical data for analysis, backtesting and trend identification.

    OHLC Data

    Retrieve Open, High, Low, Close data for any time interval.

    Market Depth

    View real-time buy and sell orders in the market depth (order book).

    Instrument Info

    Get details about instruments like lot size, tick size, expiry, and trading symbol.

    Live & Historical

    Support for both live market data and historical data retrieval.

    Real-time Quotes

    Real-time Quotes provide the current market price of a stock or instrument instantly. This helps traders and investors monitor live market movements and make timely decisions.

    Information Available

    • Last Traded Price (LTP)
    • Open Price
    • High Price
    • Low Price
    • Previous Close
    • Volume
    Example
    Reliance
    Current Price: ₹2,950

    Think of it like checking a live cricket score instead of yesterday's score.

    Historical Data

    Historical Data provides market information from the past. It allows developers and traders to analyze trends, build charts, and test trading strategies using previous market records.

    Information Available

    • Open Price
    • High Price
    • Low Price
    • Close Price
    • Volume
    Show Reliance prices from
    1 Jan 2026 to 31 Jan 2026

    Historical data is commonly used for chart analysis, backtesting, and trend identification.

    OHLC Data

    OHLC stands for Open, High, Low and Close. It summarizes price movement for a specific time interval.

    Term Description
    Open First traded price
    High Highest traded price
    Low Lowest traded price
    Close Last traded price
    Open : ₹100
    High : ₹120
    Low : ₹95
    Close : ₹115

    OHLC data is the foundation of candlestick charts and technical analysis.

    Market Depth

    Market Depth shows active buy and sell orders available in the market. It is also known as the Order Book.

    Example

    Buy Price Quantity
    ₹100 500
    ₹99 1000

    Sell Price Quantity
    ₹101 700
    ₹102 400

    Market depth helps traders understand liquidity, demand, supply and buying or selling pressure.

    Instrument Information

    Instrument Information provides complete details about a tradable security or derivative contract.

    • Trading Symbol
    • Exchange
    • Instrument Token
    • Lot Size
    • Tick Size
    • Expiry Date
    • Strike Price
    • Option Type
    Symbol: NIFTY25JUN25000CE
    Lot Size: 75
    Expiry: 25-Jun-2026

    This information is required before placing orders or identifying instruments correctly.

    Live & Historical Data

    This feature combines both live market data and historical market data through a single service.

    • Real-time Price Updates
    • Historical Records
    • Trend Analysis
    • Research & Backtesting

    Applications such as trading platforms, analytics dashboards and algorithmic trading systems commonly use both live and historical data together.
