Enums with Numeric Constants

Enums are predefined sets of values used by the Market Data API to represent options, states, or configurations — using compact numeric codes for speed and consistency.

1. ExchangeSegments

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
NSECM 1 - NSE Cash Market segment for trading equity shares.
- Used to buy and sell stocks listed on NSE.
- Example: Reliance, TCS, Infosys shares.
NSEFO 2 - NSE Futures & Options segment.
- Used for trading futures and options contracts.
- Example: NIFTY Futures, BANKNIFTY Options.
NSECD 3 - NSE Currency Derivatives segment.
- Used for trading currency futures and options.
- Example: USD/INR, EUR/INR contracts.
NSECO 4 - NSE Commodity segment.
- Used for commodity derivative trading on NSE.
- Example: Gold, Silver, Crude Oil contracts.
BSECM 11 - BSE Cash Market segment for equity trading.
- Used to buy and sell stocks listed on BSE.
- Example: Stocks traded on the Bombay Stock Exchange.
BSEFO 12 - BSE Futures & Options segment.
- Used for trading derivative contracts on BSE.
- Example: SENSEX Futures and Options.
BSECD 13 - BSE Currency Derivatives segment.
- Used for trading currency-based derivative contracts.
- Example: USD/INR currency futures.
MCXFO 51 - MCX Futures & Options segment.
- Used for commodity derivatives trading.
- Example: Gold, Silver, Crude Oil Futures.
NCDEX 21 - National Commodity & Derivatives Exchange segment.
- Used mainly for agricultural commodity trading.
- Example: Cotton, Soybean, Chana Futures.

2. MarketType

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
Normal 1 - Represents the regular trading session of the exchange.
- Market data is received for normal buy and sell activities.
- Most stock and derivative trading happens in this market type.
Oddlot 2 - Used for trades involving quantities smaller than the standard market lot.
- Commonly used when the quantity does not meet the regular lot size.
- Market data is published separately from normal trades.
Retaildebt 3 - Represents the retail debt market segment.
- Used for trading bonds and other debt securities by retail investors.
- Market data includes prices and trades of debt instruments.
Auction 4 - Represents the auction market session.
- Used for special auction-based trading conducted by the exchange.
- Orders are matched through an auction process instead of continuous trading.
CallAuction1 5 - Represents the first type of call auction market session.
- Orders are collected first and then executed together at a single price.
- Commonly used during special auction periods.
CallAuction2 6 - Represents the second type of call auction market session.
- Similar to CallAuction1, orders are matched in a batch at one price.
- Used for specific exchange-defined auction sessions.

3. InstrumentType

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
Futures 1 - A Futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price on a future date.
- Traders use it to earn profit from price movements without owning the asset immediately.
- It is commonly used for stocks, commodities, currencies, and market indices.
Options 2 - An Option gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price.
- It helps traders manage risk or profit from expected price changes.
- The buyer pays a small fee called a premium to get this right.
Spread 4 - A Spread means taking two related trading positions at the same time.
- One position is bought and the other is sold to reduce risk.
- Profit comes from the difference in price movement between the two positions.
Equity 8 - Equity represents ownership in a company through shares or stocks.
- When you buy equity shares, you become a part-owner of that company.
- Your profit can come from share price growth and dividends.
Spot 16 - Spot trading means buying or selling an asset immediately at the current market price.
- The ownership and payment are usually settled right away.
- It is the simplest form of trading used in stocks, currencies, and commodities.
PreferenceShares 32 - Preference Shares are special shares that give fixed dividends before equity shareholders are paid.
- They usually have lower risk compared to ordinary shares.
- However, preference shareholders often have limited or no voting rights.
Debentures 64 - Debentures are a type of loan given by investors to a company.
- The company promises to pay regular interest and return the money after a fixed period.
- Debenture holders are creditors, not owners of the company.
Warrants 128 - A Warrant gives the holder the right to buy company shares at a fixed price in the future.
- It is usually valid for a longer period than options.
- Investors use warrants when they expect the company's share price to rise.
Miscellaneous 256 - Miscellaneous includes financial instruments that do not fit into common categories.
- These may have unique features, purposes, or trading rules.
- Examples can vary depending on the market or financial institution.
MutualFund 512 - A Mutual Fund collects money from many investors and invests it in different assets.
- Professional fund managers make investment decisions on behalf of investors.
- It helps people diversify investments and reduce individual investment risk.

4. XTS Message Code

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
TouchLineEvent 1501 - A TouchLineEvent occurs whenever the latest market price (Last Traded Price) changes.
- It provides quick updates about the current trading activity of a stock or instrument.
- Traders use it to monitor real-time price movements and market direction.
MarketDepthEvent 1502 - A MarketDepthEvent shows the list of buy and sell orders currently available in the market.
- It helps traders see demand, supply, and liquidity at different price levels.
- This information is useful for understanding market strength and planning trades.
CandleDataEvent 1505 - A CandleDataEvent provides price data in candlestick format for a specific time period.
- Each candle contains Open, High, Low, and Close (OHLC) prices.
- Traders use candlestick data to analyze trends, patterns, and market behavior.
OpenInterestEvent 1510 - An OpenInterestEvent shows the total number of active futures or options contracts in the market.
- It indicates how much money and participation are present in a particular contract.
- Rising open interest often suggests stronger market activity and trader interest.

5. Exchange Trading Session

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
PreOpenStart 0 - This is the beginning of the pre-open market session before regular trading starts.
- During this time, traders can place, modify, or cancel orders.
- The exchange collects orders to determine the opening price.
PreOpenEnd 1 - This marks the end of the pre-open session.
- The exchange processes all collected orders and calculates the opening market price.
- Regular trading is about to begin after this phase.
NormalStart 2 - This is the start of the normal trading session.
- Buy and sell orders are matched continuously in real time.
- Most market trading activity happens during this period.
NormalEnd 4 - This indicates the end of the regular trading session.
- New trades under normal market rules stop after this point.
- The market then moves to the next session, such as post-closing.
PostClosingStart 8 - This is the beginning of the post-closing session after normal trading ends.
- Certain order types may still be accepted depending on exchange rules.
- It helps complete settlement-related or closing-price activities.
PostClosingEnd 16 - This marks the end of the post-closing session.
- All special post-market activities are completed.
- The market prepares to move into the closed state.
Closed 32 - The market is completely closed for trading.
- No new buy or sell transactions can be executed.
- This usually occurs after market hours, weekends, or holidays.
None 256 - This means no market session status is currently assigned or available.
- It may be used as a default or placeholder value in the system.
- Applications often show this when session information is unavailable.

6. Exchange Market Type

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
Normal 1 - Regular trading process jahan exchange ke standard rules follow hote hain.
- Sabhi orders normal market timings mein execute kiye jaate hain.
- Daily buying aur selling ke liye sabse commonly used category hai.
OddLot 2 - Jab quantity standard trading lot se kam ya alag hoti hai tab use kiya jaata hai.
- Aise orders special handling ke saath process hote hain.
- Small quantity transactions ke liye suitable hai.
RetailDebt 3 - Retail investors ke liye debt instruments (Bonds, NCDs, etc.) ki trading.
- Ismein fixed-income securities ko buy ya sell kiya ja sakta hai.
- Regular equity trading se alag debt market transactions ke liye use hota hai.
Auction 4 - Auction session mein pending ya unsettled positions ko resolve karne ke liye use hota hai.
- Exchange required securities ki buying ya selling auction process se karwata hai.
- Settlement obligations complete karne mein madad karta hai.
CallAuction1 5 - Pre-defined auction window jahan orders collect kiye jaate hain.
- Matching ek fixed time par hoti hai, continuously nahi.
- Price discovery aur fair order execution ke liye use kiya jaata hai.
CallAuction2 6 - Call Auction ka doosra phase ya session.
- Collected orders ko match karke final execution kiya jaata hai.
- Market mein transparency aur efficient price determination ensure karta hai.

7. CallAuction Type

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
NonCallAuction 0 - Regular market segment jahan trading continuous basis par hoti hai.
- Orders aate hi matching rules ke according execute hote hain.
- Call Auction mechanism is segment mein apply nahi hota.
PreOpen 1 - Market open hone se pehle orders collect aur match kiye jaate hain.
- Is session ka purpose opening price determine karna hota hai.
- Trading start hone se pehle fair price discovery ensure karta hai.
IPOPreOpen 2 - Naye IPO listed stock ke liye special pre-open session.
- Orders collect karke initial listing price discover ki jaati hai.
- Listing day par smooth aur transparent market opening mein madad karta hai.
RelistPreOpen 3 - Relisted security ke liye market open hone se pehle ka session.
- Orders collect karke opening price determine ki jaati hai.
- Relisting ke baad fair trading start karne mein help karta hai.
Illiquid 4 - Kam trading volume aur low market activity wali securities.
- Inmein buyers aur sellers ki availability limited ho sakti hai.
- Price movements comparatively zyada volatile ho sakte hain.
SME 5 - Small and Medium Enterprises ki listed securities ka segment.
- Yeh platform growing businesses ko capital raise karne ka mauka deta hai.
- SME companies ke shares ki trading ke liye use kiya jaata hai.
SCAS 6 - Special Call Auction Session ke under aane wali securities.
- Orders ek fixed auction process ke through collect aur match kiye jaate hain.
- Price discovery aur controlled trading environment provide karta hai.

8. BookType

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
None 0 - No special order condition is applied to the trade.
- The order follows the default market trading process.
- Used for standard buy and sell transactions.
Regular 1 - Order is placed in the normal trading market segment.
- It follows standard exchange rules and timings.
- Most day-to-day trading activities use this category.
SpecialTerms 2 - Order is subject to specific exchange-defined conditions.
- Execution may follow additional settlement or trading rules.
- Used when standard trading terms do not apply.
StopLoss 3 - Order is triggered only when a specified price is reached.
- Helps limit potential losses in volatile markets.
- Commonly used for risk management and capital protection.
Negotiated 4 - Trade is executed based on terms agreed between parties.
- Price and quantity are decided before execution.
- Typically used for special or large-value transactions.
OddLot 5 - Order quantity is smaller than the standard market lot size.
- It allows trading of non-standard share quantities.
- Commonly used for small holdings or partial transactions.
Spot 6 - Trade settlement is completed on the same day or immediately.
- Ownership transfer happens faster than regular settlement.
- Used when quick settlement is required.
Auction 7 - Order participates in an exchange auction session.
- Matching occurs through an auction-based price discovery process.
- Used for special market events and settlement requirements.
CallAuction1 11 - Orders are collected during the first call auction session.
- Execution happens at a single price after order collection.
- Helps determine a fair market price before trading.
CallAuction2 12 - Orders are collected during the second call auction session.
- Matching is performed at a calculated auction price.
- Provides additional price discovery and orderly execution.

9. OptionType

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
CA 1 - CA — Corporate Action
- Provides information about company actions such as dividends, stock splits, bonus issues, and mergers.
- Helps track changes that can affect a stock's value and shareholder holdings.
PA 2 - PA — Price Action
- Provides data about how a stock's price moves over time.
- Includes trends, price changes, highs, lows, and market movement patterns.
CE 3 - CE — Corporate Event
- Provides details about important company-related events.
- Includes earnings announcements, board meetings, annual meetings, and disclosures.
PE 4 - PE — Price Event
- Provides alerts or records of significant price-related movements.
- Includes sharp price increases, decreases, or predefined price threshold triggers.
None 5 - No specific event type is selected.
- Data will not be filtered by any event category.
- Use this option when you want the default or unfiltered market data.

10. PublishFormat

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
Binary 1 - Returns market data in a compact binary format for faster processing and lower bandwidth usage.
- Optimized for high-performance applications that require maximum speed and efficiency.
- Best suited for systems that can parse and decode binary data formats.
JSON 2 - Returns market data in a human-readable JSON format.
- Easy to integrate, debug, and use across most programming languages and applications.
- Best suited for development, testing, and standard API integrations.

11. BroadCastMode

Enum Name Numeric Code Description
Full 1 - Returns the complete set of available market data for the requested instrument or event.
- Includes all relevant fields and details without any data reduction.
- Best suited when comprehensive information is required for analysis or reporting.
Partial 2 - Returns only a subset of the available market data.
- Includes essential fields to reduce response size and improve performance.
- Best suited when only key information is needed for faster processing.

12. Socket Event

Enum Name Description
joined - WebSocket Joined
- Sent when a client successfully connects and joins the WebSocket channel.
- Confirms that the real-time data stream is active and ready.
error - Error Event
- Indicates a failure during WebSocket connection or request processing.
- Includes invalid request, authentication failure, or system error.
warning - Warning Message
- Non-critical advisory message from the server.
- Example: rate limit approaching or deprecated API usage detected.
success - Success Response
- Confirms that a client request was processed successfully.
- Example: subscription to an instrument acknowledged.
