    Holdings

    Holdings are the stocks or securities that belong to you and are stored in your demat/investment account. These are your long-term investments and remain until you sell them.

    Demat Account Stocks
    Live P&L Tracking
    Delivery Only
    Long-term Investment

    What are Holdings?

    Holdings represent the shares that you own in your Demat account after successful delivery. Unlike positions, holdings do not include intraday trades — they only show shares that have been settled and delivered to your Demat account.

    How Holdings Work

    When you buy shares for delivery (CNC), the shares are credited to your Demat account on the settlement day. Once credited, they appear in your Holdings and you can:

    Days
    Hold for a few days
    Months
    Hold for several months
    Years
    Hold for many years

    There is no requirement to sell them on the same day — unlike intraday positions.

    Information Available in Holdings

    FieldDescription
    SymbolStock name or trading symbol
    ISINUnique identification number assigned to the security
    ExchangeExchange where the stock is traded (NSE / BSE)
    QuantityTotal shares currently owned
    AvailableQuantityShares that can be sold immediately
    AveragePriceAverage purchase price per share
    LTPLast Traded Price — current market price of the stock
    MarketValueCurrent value of your holdings (Quantity × LTP)
    InvestedValueTotal amount invested (Quantity × Average Price)
    UnrealizedPnLProfit or loss if you sold the shares at the current market price
    DayChangeToday's gain or loss based on today's market movement

    Understanding Key Fields

    Quantity

    Number of shares you own.

    Reliance Industries
    Quantity = 50 shares owned
    Average Price

    Average price at which you bought the shares.

    Buy 20 @ ₹100 + Buy 30 @ ₹120
    (20×100 + 30×120) ÷ 50 = ₹112
    Market Value

    Current worth of your holdings.

    Market Value = Qty × LTP
    100 × ₹550 = ₹55,000
    Invested Value

    Total money spent to purchase the shares.

    Invested Value = Qty × Avg Price
    100 × ₹500 = ₹50,000
    Unrealized P&L

    Profit or loss if you sold now. Called unrealized because you haven't sold yet.

    P&L = Market Value − Invested Value
    ₹55,000 − ₹50,000 = +₹5,000 profit
    ₹48,000 − ₹50,000 = −₹2,000 loss
    Day Change

    Gain or loss during the current trading day.

    Yesterday close: ₹540 → Today LTP: ₹550
    Day Change = +₹10 × 100 shares = +₹1,000

    Lifecycle of Holdings

    Buy Delivery Shares
    ↓
    Order Executed
    ↓
    Settlement Completed
    ↓
    Shares Credited to Demat
    ↓
    Appear in Holdings
    ↓
    Keep or Sell Anytime

    Holdings vs Positions

    HOLDINGS
    • ▸ Shows stocks owned in the Demat account
    • ▸ Only delivery shares are displayed
    • ▸ Shares remain until sold
    • ▸ Used mainly for investing
    • ▸ Cannot have a negative quantity
    POSITIONS
    • ▸ Shows active trading positions
    • ▸ Includes intraday, delivery, futures, options
    • ▸ May close on the same day or remain open
    • ▸ Used for tracking trading activity
    • ▸ Can be long (positive) or short (negative)

    Real-Life Example

    You buy 100 shares of XYZ Ltd. at ₹200 for delivery. A few months later, the stock rises to ₹260.

    FieldValue
    Quantity100
    AveragePrice₹200
    LTP₹260
    InvestedValue₹20,000
    MarketValue₹26,000
    UnrealizedProfit+₹6,000

    If you later sell all 100 shares, the holdings for XYZ Ltd. become 0, and the profit becomes realized.

    Why Holdings Are Important

    Track the stocks you currently own
    Monitor current value of your investments
    View unrealized profit or loss
    Decide when to buy more or sell
    Build a long-term investment portfolio

    In Simple Words: Holdings are the delivery shares that you own in your Demat account. They show what stocks you currently own, how much you invested, their current market value, and whether you are in profit or loss.

    Get Holdings API

    Fetch all holdings for the authenticated user from their Demat account.

    ENDPOINT GET
    https://xts.rmoneyindia.co.in:3000/interactive/portfolio/holdings
    Requires: valid Authorization token

    Request Body Parameters

    Parameter Name Type Mandatory Description
    clientID ClientID N Client ID — mandatory in case of Dealer

    Response Body Parameters

    Parameter Name Type Description
    ClientIdUserIDUser specific identification
    RMSHoldingListRMSHoldingListList of RMS holding records for the client
    HoldingsHoldingsArray of individual holding entries
    ISINISINThe standard ISIN representing stocks listed on multiple exchanges
    RMSHoldingIdRMSHoldingIdUnique holding ID
    ExchangeNSEInstrumentIdExchangeInstrumentIDExchange scrip code or symbol token — unique identifier for NSE
    ExchangeBSEInstrumentIdExchangeInstrumentIDExchange scrip code or symbol token — unique identifier for BSE
    ExchangeMSEInstrumentIdExchangeInstrumentIDExchange scrip code or symbol token — unique identifier for MSE
    HoldingTypeHoldingTypeRepresents holding type — see HoldingTypes enum
    HoldingQuantityQuantityTotal holding quantity
    CollateralValuationTypeValuationTypeValuation type used for collateral calculation
    HaircutHaircutPercentage reduction applied to the market value of a security when calculating its collateral value
    CollateralQuantityQuantityQuantity placed as collateral with the broker
    CreatedByCreatedByUser or system identifier that created the holding record
    LastUpdatedByLastUpdatedByUser or system identifier that last modified the holding record
    CreatedOnCreatedOnDate and time when the holding record was first created
    LastUpdatedOnLastUpdatedOnDate and time when the holding record was last updated
    UsedQuantityQuantityQuantity sold from the net holding quantity
    IsCollateralHoldingBooleanIndicates whether the holding is being used as collateral for margin or leverage
    BuyAvgPricePriceAverage price at which all shares were bought
    IsBuyAvgPriceProvidedBooleanIndicates whether the buy average price for the holding has been provided
    AuthorizeQuantityQuantityQuantity authorized at the depository for sale
    IsNeedToDeleteBooleanIndicates whether the holding record is marked for removal from the system
    CollateralHoldingListCollateralHoldingListList of collateral holdings associated with this record
    HoldingsHoldingsNested holdings list within the collateral holding
