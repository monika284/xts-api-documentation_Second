Holdings

Holdings are the stocks or securities that belong to you and are stored in your demat/investment account. These are your long-term investments and remain until you sell them.

Demat Account Stocks
Live P&L Tracking
Delivery Only
Long-term Investment

What are Holdings?

Holdings represent the shares that you own in your Demat account after successful delivery. Unlike positions, holdings do not include intraday trades — they only show shares that have been settled and delivered to your Demat account.

How Holdings Work

When you buy shares for delivery (CNC), the shares are credited to your Demat account on the settlement day. Once credited, they appear in your Holdings and you can:

Days
Hold for a few days
Months
Hold for several months
Years
Hold for many years

There is no requirement to sell them on the same day — unlike intraday positions.

Understanding Key Fields

Quantity

Number of shares you own.

Reliance Industries
Quantity = 50 shares owned
Average Price

Average price at which you bought the shares.

Buy 20 @ ₹100 + Buy 30 @ ₹120
(20×100 + 30×120) ÷ 50 = ₹112
Market Value

Current worth of your holdings.

Market Value = Qty × LTP
100 × ₹550 = ₹55,000
Invested Value

Total money spent to purchase the shares.

Invested Value = Qty × Avg Price
100 × ₹500 = ₹50,000
Unrealized P&L

Profit or loss if you sold now. Called unrealized because you haven't sold yet.

P&L = Market Value − Invested Value
₹55,000 − ₹50,000 = +₹5,000 profit
₹48,000 − ₹50,000 = −₹2,000 loss
Day Change

Gain or loss during the current trading day.

Yesterday close: ₹540 → Today LTP: ₹550
Day Change = +₹10 × 100 shares = +₹1,000

Holdings vs Positions

HOLDINGS
  • ▸ Shows stocks owned in the Demat account
  • ▸ Only delivery shares are displayed
  • ▸ Shares remain until sold
  • ▸ Used mainly for investing
  • ▸ Cannot have a negative quantity
POSITIONS
  • ▸ Shows active trading positions
  • ▸ Includes intraday, delivery, futures, options
  • ▸ May close on the same day or remain open
  • ▸ Used for tracking trading activity
  • ▸ Can be long (positive) or short (negative)

Why Holdings Are Important

Track the stocks you currently own
Monitor current value of your investments
View unrealized profit or loss
Decide when to buy more or sell
Build a long-term investment portfolio

In Simple Words: Holdings are the delivery shares that you own in your Demat account. They show what stocks you currently own, how much you invested, their current market value, and whether you are in profit or loss.

Get Holdings API

Fetch all holdings for the authenticated user from their Demat account.

ENDPOINT GET
https://xts.rmoneyindia.co.in:3000/interactive/portfolio/holdings
Requires: valid Authorization token
