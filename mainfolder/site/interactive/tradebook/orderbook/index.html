Order Book

A complete record of all buy and sell orders placed in your trading account — including their current status, execution details, and full order lifecycle.

All Order Statuses
Real-time Updates
Modify & Cancel
All Segments

What is an Order Book?

The Order Book is a record of all the orders you have placed with your trading account. It shows the current status of each order — whether it is pending, executed, cancelled, rejected, or partially filled. An Order Book helps you track every order from the moment it is placed until it is completed or cancelled.
Simple Definition: An Order Book is a list of all your buy and sell orders, along with their current status and details.

Why is the Order Book Important?

Track all placed orders
Check if order is executed or pending
View order price & quantity details
Modify or cancel eligible orders
Monitor partially executed orders
Avoid missed or duplicate orders

How an Order Moves Through the Order Book

1
Place Order
2
Order Received by Exchange
3
Order Pending — waiting to match
4
Matched with Another Order
✓
Order Executed → Appears in Trade Book & Position

If the order cannot be executed, it may instead become:

Cancelled — user or broker cancelled before execution
Rejected — exchange or broker rejected the order

Common Order Statuses

PENDING

The order has been accepted but not yet executed.

Buy 100 shares @ ₹500
Current Market Price = ₹505
Status: Pending
FILLED

The entire order has been successfully executed.

Buy 100 shares @ ₹500
Filled Quantity = 100
Status: Filled
PARTIALLY FILLED

Only part of the requested quantity has been executed.

Buy Quantity = 100  |  Executed = 60
Remaining = 40
Status: Partially Filled
CANCELLED / REJECTED

Cancelled by user/broker, or rejected by exchange due to invalid price, quantity, or insufficient funds.

Buy 100 shares — cancelled before execution
Reasons: Insufficient funds · Invalid price · Market closed
Status: Cancelled / Rejected

Order Book vs Trade Book vs Position

ORDER BOOK
  • ▸Shows all placed orders
  • ▸Includes pending, cancelled & filled
  • ▸Tracks order lifecycle
TRADE BOOK
  • ▸Shows only executed trades
  • ▸Only completed executions
  • ▸Confirms completed trades
POSITION
  • ▸Current open quantity
  • ▸Only executed quantities
  • ▸Tracks current holdings

Real-Life Example

Rahul places an order to buy 100 shares of ABC Ltd. at ₹500.

1
Order appears in Order Book — Status: Pending
2
Exchange executes 60 shares — Status: Partially Filled
3
Remaining 40 shares are executed — Status: Filled
✓
Trade appears in Trade Book and Rahul's Position shows 100 shares

In Simple Words: The Order Book is a complete record of all your buy and sell orders. It shows each order's details and current status — Pending, Filled, Partially Filled, Cancelled, or Rejected — so you can monitor every order from placement to completion.
