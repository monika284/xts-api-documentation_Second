    Exchange Messages

    Exchange Messages are official notifications sent by the stock exchange (such as NSE or BSE) to inform traders and brokers about important market events, trading updates, or system announcements.

    Official Notifications
    Real-time Alerts
    NSE & BSE
    Market Transparency

    What are Exchange Messages?

    Exchange Messages are official announcements from the exchange that provide important information related to trading, market operations, and system updates. They help users stay informed about anything that may affect trading activities.
    OFFICIAL SOURCE
    Directly from NSE, BSE, or other exchanges
    REAL-TIME
    Delivered instantly when the exchange publishes them
    ACTIONABLE
    Help traders make informed decisions instantly

    Why are Exchange Messages Important?

    Stay informed about market changes as they happen
    Know if trading is suspended or resumed for a stock
    Be aware of special trading sessions or extended hours
    Receive important operational updates from the exchange
    Avoid trading issues caused by exchange announcements
    Improve overall market transparency and trader confidence

    Common Types of Exchange Messages

    1. Trading Halt

    The exchange temporarily stops trading for a particular stock or the entire market.

    NSE Notice:
    Trading in ABC Ltd. has been
    suspended until further notice.
    2. Trading Resume

    The exchange announces that trading has started again after a halt.

    Trading in ABC Ltd. has resumed
    from 11:30 AM.
    3. Market Holiday Notice

    The exchange informs participants about upcoming market holidays.

    The stock market will remain
    closed on 15th August due to
    Independence Day.
    4. Corporate Action

    Information about company events — dividend, bonus shares, stock split, rights issue, merger.

    XYZ Ltd. has announced
    a 1:1 Bonus Issue.
    5. Auction Notification

    The exchange announces auction sessions for unsettled trades.

    Auction session will begin
    at 2:00 PM.
    6. System Maintenance

    The exchange notifies users about planned maintenance windows.

    Exchange systems will undergo
    maintenance from 6 PM to 8 PM.
    7. Price Band Revision

    The exchange changes the upper or lower trading limits for a security.

    The price band for ABC Ltd.
    has been revised from 10% to 20%.
    8. Market-Wide Circuit Breaker

    The exchange pauses all trading due to significant index movements.

    Trading halted for 45 minutes
    due to a market-wide circuit breaker.

    Exchange Message Flow

    Exchange (NSE / BSE)
    ▼
    Generates Official Announcement
    ▼
    Broker / API Receives Message
    ▼
    Trading Platform Displays Message
    ▼
    Trader Reads the Notification

    Real-Life Example

    You are planning to buy shares of ABC Ltd. Before placing your order, you receive this exchange message:

    ⚠ NSE Alert:
    Trading in ABC Ltd. has been suspended
    due to a pending corporate announcement.
    Since trading is temporarily halted, you cannot place or execute orders for that stock until the exchange resumes trading. The exchange message helps you avoid a failed order attempt and plan accordingly.

    Exchange Messages in Market Data API

    The Exchange Messages endpoint allows applications to retrieve official messages published by the exchange and display them directly to traders.

    Message Type
    Description
    Trading Halt
    Trading suspension notices for stocks or markets
    Trading Resume
    Trading restart announcements after a halt
    Market Holiday
    Exchange holiday notifications and closure dates
    Corporate Actions
    Dividend, bonus, stock split, rights issue, etc.
    Auction Notices
    Auction session timings and information
    System Maintenance
    Exchange maintenance windows and downtime
    Price Band Updates
    Changes in circuit limits or price bands
    General Announcements
    Other important exchange notifications

    In Simple Words: Exchange Messages are official announcements from the stock exchange that inform traders about important market events such as trading halts, market holidays, corporate actions, auctions, and system updates. They help traders stay informed and make better trading decisions.

    Exchange Message GET

    The exchange sends various messages to connected brokers, such as information about bans, circuit limits, and news about listed companies. Users can access messages relevant to them using this API.

    URL

    GET https://xts.rmoneyindia.co.in:3000/1interactive/messages/exchange?exchangeSegment=NSECM

    Request Body Parameters

    Parameter Name Type Mandatory Description
    exchangeSegment ExchangeSegment ↗ Y ExchangeSegment

    Response Body Parameters

    Parameter Name Type Description
    exchangeSegment ExchangeSegment ↗ ExchangeSegment ↗
    ExchangeTimeStamp ExchangeTimeStamp ↗ Date and time provided by the exchange when a specific event or message was generated.
    BroadcastMessage BroadcastMessage Message Send by exchange

    Response Body JSON

    {
      "type": "success",
      "code": "s-messages-0001",
      "description": "Exchange messages",
      "result": {
        "messageList": [
          {
            "ExchangeSegment": 2,
            "ExchangeTimeStamp": 1452777915,
            "BroadcastMessage": "Listing -> CORPORATE ANNOUNCEMENTS",
            "SequenceId": 1904678908069178,
            "ExchangeInstrumentID": 0,
            "MessageCode": 1506,
            "MessageVersion": 8,
            "TokenID": 0,
            "ApplicationType": 0,
            "SequenceNumber": 1904678908069177
          }
        ]
      }
    }
